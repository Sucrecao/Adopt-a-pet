The user continues to require realistic plush cat, dog and parrot companions,
with one complete animal picture per displayed pose, smooth motion and healthy

## Evidence and separate acceptance gates

Finite native baseline:18,001 refresh callbacks on120Hz and60Hz displays,
no interval greater than1.5 times median. Source holds, discontinuous poses and
integer native travel still cause stepped pictures. Successful-pose caching and
changed-value DOM writes reduce redundant work; they do not fix source anatomy.

Four-photo and single-next-photo cat/dog pilots preserve complete characters
but fail measured planted contact. The cat single next photo moves its near
forepaw12.856px forward and6.737px upward instead of0.171/0.759 at canonical256;
far forepaw world drift is5.621px. Do not expand rejected pilots into128 pictures.

Source acceptance requires the entire character, stable identity/camera/lighting,
continuous limb identity, naturally alternating weight-bearing contacts, no
visible planted-foot sliding, no fur/face flicker, no artificial motion blur,
and matching loop and action junctions. Inspect every actual source picture,
every adjacent edge, loop edge and entry/exit edge in normal and slow playback.
Numeric measurements screen candidates and do not replace visual judgment.
Occluded feet are recorded as occluded; inferred locations cannot pass an
observed-contact check.

Runtime acceptance requires exactly one approved source photo per draw, correct
distance/pose relation, preparation readiness, freeze/reversal behavior, bounded
decoded memory, healthy finite native refresh timing on60/120Hz, and no source
readback in the playback loop. Report repeat poses, skipped source phases and
long refresh intervals separately. No average FPS or number of images certifies
silky motion.

## Temporal source route

First generate one continuous full-character kitten walking video from an
observed complete plush reference. Use a fixed three-quarter camera and stable
subject framing, a natural four-beat walk with low rounded paw recovery, stable
fur and lighting, and clean background suitable for provider-supported subject
matting. A genuinely continuous video is preferred to unrelated picture calls.
Connection and actual tool capabilities must be verified before generation.

Inspect the original video before background removal or frame extraction.
Extract each actual source frame with exact timestamps and source provenance;
do not synthesize missing video frames. Uniform whole-picture registration may
stabilize camera translation but cannot repair faulty foot placement. Keep every
raw output and rejected source. Source cadence below the display rate must be
reported honestly; seek a better temporal source if it visibly steps.

Validate one complete recovery, then a full cycle and seam. Derive stride and
per-phase travel from observed supporting soles rather than assigning an
arbitrary cadence. Only then generate dog/parrot and complete-photo junctions
for idle, turn, pet, feed, groom, stretch, hop and rest. Final state density depends
on its duration and inspected source motion; do not promise a metadata count.

The existing preview remains an original-source explorer until new source
passes. Candidate media are marked unapproved. Update the actual app/preview
and package only after source and runtime gates both pass.
