# Local complete-character source pilot

The user chose continued local inspection after Runway video was unavailable on the connected Free plan. Existing sources fail contact continuity. This reversible experiment tests whether one persistent three-dimensional character can supply genuinely continuous complete pictures while retaining the current silver kitten's realistic plush appearance.

The offline scene, geometry, skin, fur, eyes, materials, illumination and camera remain the same across poses. Every exported source is a complete RGBA camera render. The app continues to choose complete pictures; no bones, body-part photos, mesh warping or adjacent-image blending enter the app. Existing production photographs, metadata and releases remain unchanged until actual appearance and motion acceptance.

First render one neutral complete character. Compare the original v2 neutral at 256 and enlarged sizes, using `.artifacts/whole-local-3d-cat-appearance-contract.md`. Reject different identity, ball joints, clay/plastic skin, floating eyes, insufficient short fur, broken leg roots, missing negative space, wrong head/ear/tail proportions or clipped features. Only a sufficiently faithful appearance permits a limited gait pilot.

For gait, use the candidate coordinate and contact contract in `.artifacts/whole-local-3d-cat-gait-contract.md`: +X forward, +Z up, near −Y; fixed bone lengths; three stance paws, low recovery; world-fixed contacts; continuous toe release and C2 recovery; fixed ortho projection whose +X maps only to screen X. Small sensor roll is a visible tradeoff to review. The camera follows ground-root X only, and lighting stays fixed relative to the character's ground root. Fur seeds and rest material coordinates remain unchanged.

The authoring process may use a skeleton and a connected skin. This is a new complete scene, not the prior two-dimensional photograph-part experiments. No old body or limb bitmap becomes a model texture. All model code and raw renders live under `.artifacts/whole-local-3d-cat-pilot`, with tool/model/seed/phase/camera hashes and source provenance.

After appearance acceptance, render a small phase sample, then 128 unique complete poses plus phase 1 solely for seam diagnosis. Check every actual PNG and adjacent/wrap edge, whole-size playback, observed visible sole contacts with compensated travel, occlusions, silhouette, short-fur stability and face consistency. Mathematical landmarks and rendered file counts do not certify the visible result. Native source selection and accepted travel need separate verification before any production integration.

If the model cannot retain the approved plush identity, reject it and keep its diagnostic evidence. Do not promote it merely because the renderer works or its contacts are deterministic.
